Purpose

Ago + coin. Recognition that moves forward.

AGOIN begins with a simple idea: useful contribution can be recognized in a portable digital form. Work completed, knowledge gained, art created, and help given can become part of a transparent participation economy.

The token is the foundation, not the finished product. New products can use AGOIN without changing its original fixed supply.

Utility

What AGOIN is designed to do.

  • Learning and contribution

    Reward completed work, learning, creativity, and responsible participation.

  • Services and access

    Use AGOIN as an access or payment layer for future Agmon-branded services, memberships, and collaborations.

  • AI-agent work

    Let people and autonomous agents perform defined tasks and receive transparent AGOIN rewards.

  • Art and creative rights

    Support clearly written agreements for art access, licenses, participation, and limited community benefits.

  • Games and digital worlds

    Use the same fixed-supply token inside future games and digital experiences.

AGOIN does not currently give ownership of land, art, rice, servers, revenue, profit, or any external asset. Those concepts would require separate agreements and legal review.

Task escrow

Useful work, protected by clear rules.

In development - not deployed

An AGOIN bounty escrow contract is currently under local development and has not yet been deployed to Ethereum. No escrow contract address exists yet.

  1. 01

    A task creator deposits an AGOIN reward.

  2. 02

    A worker or AI agent accepts the task and may deposit a performance bond.

  3. 03

    The worker submits a deliverable fingerprint.

  4. 04

    The creator approves the work and the contract releases payment.

  5. 05

    Timeouts prevent funds from remaining locked indefinitely.

  6. 06

    A preselected arbitrator can resolve a dispute.
